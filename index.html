Welcome to Kent Sandwich on Yanushkevich

Kent Sandwich is a beloved neighborhood sandwich shop situated in the vibrant student district of Astana at Adolf Yanushkevich Street, 1, situated directly opposite the ENU campus. Established to fulfill the need for wholesome, freshly prepared hot meals between academic lectures, our grill kitchen transforms simple ingredients into golden, crispy toasted masterpieces served within minutes.

Unlike standard fast-food chains that rely on pre-assembled cold sandwiches, every single item at Kent Sandwich is built to order and toasted under double-sided contact press grills heated to precisely 220°C. This high-heat grilling locks in rich flavors, melts our signature cheddar cheese into the tender fillings, and gives our sandwich bread its hallmark crunchy texture that students across the capital recognize instantly.

The Secret of Our Toasted Sandwiches

Great street food starts with uncompromised ingredient freshness and disciplined grilling technique inspired by Mediterranean panini traditions. Our kitchen follows three fundamental craftsmanship standards every morning before the opening bell rings:

Artisan Toast Bread
Thick-cut, locally baked bakery loaves designed to withstand high pressure without absorbing excess moisture.
House-Made Herb & Garlic Sauce
Our proprietary cold-emulsion dressing blending aromatic garlic, pickled dill relish, and light cultured yogurt.
100% Halal Meats & Real Dairy
Daily deliveries of farm poultry breast and tender lean beef marinated in mild paprika and black pepper.

We pride ourselves on offering full-sized hot sandwiches starting from just 640 ₸, making balanced nutrition truly accessible. For smaller snacks during short intervals, our compact Mini Sandwich option delivers the same satisfying crunch for only 390 ₸.
