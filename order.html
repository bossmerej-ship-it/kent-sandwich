Express Takeaway Pre-Ordering

Welcome to the digital pre-order service for Kent Sandwich at Adolf Yanushkevich 1, located steps away from the ENU campus. To accommodate tight study schedules, lunch breaks, and exam intervals, our express pre-ordering system lets you submit your food request in advance so your hot sandwich is fresh off the grill the moment you arrive.

Our kitchen initiates toasting precisely 7 minutes prior to your scheduled arrival time. All hot sandwiches are wrapped in heat-retaining thermal aluminum paper to preserve optimal crunch and melted cheese texture.
