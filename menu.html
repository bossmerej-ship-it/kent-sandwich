Fresh Fast-Casual Grille Menu

Every sandwich served at our Adolf Yanushkevich counter is prepared using fresh ingredients, seasoned halal poultry and beef, melted cheddar cheese, and crisp garden vegetables. We toast our sandwiches to order on heavy cast-iron dual contact grills, ensuring an unbeatable crunch that stays hot during your walk across the university campus.

Check our complete offerings below. Guests can customize any sandwich with extra cheese toppings, spicy jalapeño peppers, or our house garlic dip.

How to Build Your Custom Meal

Follow our straightforward ordering formula for dine-in or express takeout:

  • Step 1: Select your main protein & filling
    • Marinated tender chicken breast
    • Seasoned slow-cooked minced beef
    • Smoked turkey ham with double mozzarella
  • Step 2: Choose your signature dressing
    • House Herb & Garlic Cream Sauce
    • Spicy Smoked Paprika Dressing
    • Classic Creamy Mayonnaise & Mustard
  • Step 3: Add sides and refreshments

Kitchen Preparation Protocol

  1. Order ticket printed at the kitchen display screen
  2. Sandwich layered with chilled ingredients on bakery bread
  3. Double contact grill pressed for exactly 4 to 5 minutes until core reaches 75°C
  4. Sandwich sliced diagonally, wrapped in thermal foil, and delivered hot
