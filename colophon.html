Design choices

Why the site looks this way

  1. Colours. Black, warm white and yellow match the shop’s packaging and interior.
  2. Structure. Headings, lists, tables, figures and fieldsets keep the content organised.
  3. Layout. Bootstrap columns stack on phones, split at md and expand at lg. Navigation collapses below 992px.
  4. Forms. Native controls support selection, validation and reset. Sending is disabled because no online service is connected.
