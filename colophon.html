Design choices

Why the site looks this way

  1. Colours. Black, warm white and yellow echo the shop’s packaging and interior.
  2. Structure. Headings, lists, tables, figures and fieldsets organise the information. Every content photograph has descriptive alternative text.
  3. Layout. Bootstrap columns stack on phones and split at md and lg. The navigation collapses below 992px.
  4. Feedback. Required fields are checked by the browser. Completing the form shows a local confirmation; it does not send anything to the shop.
  5. Keyboard access. The skip link reaches the main content, links and controls have visible focus, and official contacts remain ordinary links.
