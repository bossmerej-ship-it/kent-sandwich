Design choices

Why the site looks this way

  1. Colours. Black, warm white and yellow match the shop’s packaging and interior.
  2. Structure. Headings, lists, tables, figures and fieldsets keep the content organised.
  3. Layout. The pages are made for a normal desktop window. Responsive styles can be added later.
  4. Forms. The order and feedback forms are ready for JavaScript, but they do not send data yet.
