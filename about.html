Our place

Hot sandwiches in the centre of Astana

Kent Sandwich is a small sandwich shop in the Tselinny microdistrict. You can eat inside or take your order with you.

The format

Simple enough to remember

Kent Sandwich serves toasted sandwiches in kraft paper sleeves. The packaging is simple, easy to carry and matches the shop’s black and yellow style.
